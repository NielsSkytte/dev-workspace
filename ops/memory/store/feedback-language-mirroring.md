**Do not switch language unless Niels does.** He writes to me in English; the reply, and
crucially the **option labels in a closed question**, stay English until he writes Danish.

**Why:** 2026-08-13 (Carl Ras / datahub). Closed questions were being posed with Danish
labels - `"ja"`, `"vent"`, `"kun outbound"`, `"i atomic"`, `"lokalt"` - inside otherwise
English replies, in a session conducted entirely in English. Niels: *"you are starting to
ask me in danish, while not an issue dont switch langueage unless i do."*

The pull comes from the customer context: Carl Ras is `language: da`, and customer-facing
**artefacts** (mails, SoWs, documents, briefs) are Danish by default. That default governs
**deliverables addressed to the customer**, not the working conversation with Niels.

**How to apply:** all agents, all sessions. Mirror the language of the user's last message.
A customer's `language:` field sets the language of things written *for that customer*; it
never sets the language of the reply to Niels. If a deliverable should be Danish and the
conversation is English, write the deliverable in Danish and describe it in English.
